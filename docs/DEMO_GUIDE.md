# RepForge — roughly two-minute demonstration

**Purpose:** show a salesperson learning from a specific conversational mistake,
not simply chatting with an AI. Two minutes is a recording target, not an enforced
session duration or a guarantee of provider response time.

Use the **updated preview** for this version:
https://prospect-practice.preview.emergentagent.com

The portfolio's live link is https://www.therepforge.app/; it was reachable but still
served older landing copy during this pass. No deployment was performed.

## Before recording

1. Open an incognito browser window. Disable desktop notifications; close personal tabs.
2. Use fictional demo data only. Do not enter employer secrets, customer names, passwords,
   financial details or actual pipeline information. Hide browser bookmarks and account email.
3. Use a current microphone-capable browser over HTTPS. Check headset volume and permission.
4. For a reliable silent demonstration choose **Use typed practice**. If recording voice,
   grant microphone access, speak to check input activity, test the buyer and explicitly
   confirm that you heard it. A sound meter does not prove transcription accuracy.
5. Set your screen recorder to capture the browser tab, microphone and system audio only
   if supported and wanted. Make a ten-second local test and listen back yourself.
6. Do not reveal provider dashboards, environment files, developer console or session storage.

## Walkthrough and narration

| Target | Screen/action | Suggested narration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing → **Try a 2-minute demo** | “New reps often know sales concepts but haven't practised enough conversations. Managers cannot role-play with everyone on demand.” |
| 0:15–0:30 | Read product, Jordan's role and objective; choose mic or text | “RepForge is a sales flight simulator: a realistic buyer, a clear situation and a safe place to make mistakes.” |
| 0:30–1:00 | Complete two or three exchanges | “The prospect reacts to what I actually say. I'm deliberately moving into my pitch too early here so we can see what the coach catches.” |
| 1:00–1:20 | **End call**; allow grading to finish | “The call is frozen before grading. Coaching must cite the saved conversation rather than hand me a generic score.” |
| 1:20–1:40 | Show coach observation, supporting quotation and applicable skill scores | “Here's the specific opportunity I missed, why it mattered and a better question to try.” |
| 1:40–2:00+ | **Retry this exact moment**, choose input, give a better response | “I can replay this situation as a coached drill. The original assessment remains unchanged, and a short drill isn't compared with an entire call.” |

If latency pushes the recording past two minutes, keep the honest wait or visibly label
an edit as “waiting for AI response.” Do not splice in a fabricated score or imply that
a different attempt's report belongs to the visible conversation.

## Fictional practice lines

Use these as prompts, not a rigid transcript; the buyer's replies vary.

- Opening: “Hi Jordan, I help sales teams replace disconnected tools with a CRM.
  Have you got a minute?”
- Deliberately weak follow-up: “Our platform has lots of features and can solve all
  your problems. Could I show you a demo tomorrow?”
- Retry improvement: respond to the actual selected miss. For a discovery miss:
  “Before we talk about features, what's the biggest problem with your current tools,
  and what does that cost your team each week?”

A weak attempt should receive weak coaching. Do not promise a particular score or
“improvement”; a tie is correctly reported as no higher targeted score.

## Recovery and honest boundaries

- No microphone/denied permission: choose typed practice; nothing should falsely say
  audio was verified.
- Prospect/voice failure: saved text remains available. Retry the specific failed reply
  when offered, or continue with typed input; there is no hidden generic browser voice.
- Grading failure: **Retry grading** reuses the frozen transcript without reopening speech.
- Unused active preparation: abandon it through History to release a session slot.
- Sample report: public, fictional and read-only. It does not offer audio replay or
  measured vocal-cue analysis.
